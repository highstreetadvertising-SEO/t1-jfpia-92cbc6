What We Do

At John Forsythe Personal Injury Attorney, we understand that being involved in a car accident can be one of the most overwhelming and life-altering experiences a person can face. From mounting medical bills and lost wages to physical pain and emotional distress, the aftermath of a serious collision can leave victims feeling helpless and uncertain about their future. Our firm exists to shoulder that burden on your behalf, guiding you through the complexities of the legal process while you focus on healing and recovery. We are committed to ensuring that every client receives the individualized attention and strategic legal counsel their case deserves.

Attorney John Forsythe has built a reputation throughout Puyallup and the surrounding Washington communities for his tenacious approach to personal injury litigation and his unwavering dedication to client advocacy. With extensive experience handling car accident claims, he possesses a deep understanding of Washington state traffic laws, insurance company tactics, and the negotiation strategies needed to secure maximum compensation. Whether your case involves a rear-end collision, a multi-vehicle accident, a drunk driving incident, or an intersection crash, John Forsythe has the knowledge and skill to effectively fight for your rights both at the negotiating table and in the courtroom.

Our firm handles all aspects of your personal injury claim from start to finish, including gathering evidence, consulting with medical experts, calculating the full extent of your damages, and negotiating directly with insurance companies on your behalf. We work on a contingency fee basis, which means you pay absolutely nothing unless we win your case. This approach ensures that every injured person in Puyallup and the surrounding areas has access to high-quality legal representation, regardless of their financial situation. We believe that justice should never be out of reach simply because of economic hardship.

What sets John Forsythe Personal Injury Attorney apart from other law firms is the genuine care and personal commitment that Attorney Forsythe brings to each and every case. Clients are never treated as just a case number — they are treated as people with real needs, real families, and real futures at stake. Attorney Forsythe takes the time to thoroughly understand each client's unique circumstances, maintain open communication throughout the legal process, and craft a legal strategy tailored specifically to their situation. This client-first philosophy has earned the firm a strong reputation for trust, integrity, and consistent results throughout the Puyallup community.
